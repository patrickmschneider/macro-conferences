Coverage & how dates are checked

This is an initial release with broad macro coverage in development. It includes established conferences and specialist workshops, plus household finance and pension research. Inclusion is based on academic programmes and organisers, not a numerical prestige score.

Dates link to primary announcements. Daily collection is the target; blocked or changed sources are flagged. A failed check retains the last verified record. Conflicting dates are withheld from open calls. Always check the organiser’s page before submitting.

Automated publication currently uses conservative CEPR and NBER rules. Other sources are monitored for leads; some flagship series still need adapters. “Series” distinguishes verified research sources from coverage leads. Calendar refresh frequency depends on your calendar provider.
